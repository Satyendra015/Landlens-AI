Section 01 • The Problem Statement

The National Crisis in Land Revenue Administration

1. Historical & Degraded Physical Paper Registers

Millions of Jamabandi, Khasra, Khatauni, and e-Stamp deeds locked away in aged, yellowed, low-contrast physical registers over 50 years old. Faded ink and torn paper make manual transcription extremely error-prone.

2. Extreme Regional Format & Vernacular Fragmentation

Inconsistent schemas and languages across 28 states (7/12 in Maharashtra, RoR/Jamabandi in MP, Kaveri in Karnataka, e-Stamp in UP). Commercial cloud OCR tools fail on Indian legal deed structures.

3. Manual Transcription Bottlenecks & Title Fraud

Manual clerical entry produces duplicate registration claims, unnoticed spelling manipulations, fraudulent mutations, and disconnected geospatial boundary errors.

Section 03 • Core Intelligence

Pillars 1 & 2: Computer Vision Pipeline & Devanagari OCR

PILLAR 1: ADAPTIVE COMPUTER VISION PIPELINE
  • Laplacian Blur Metric: Computes variance of Laplacian to automatically flag blurry scans.
  • Bilateral Filtering: Eliminates scanner speckles and paper yellowing without softening character edges.
  • CLAHE Enhancement: Rescues faded, degraded 50-year-old ink entries by equalizing local histogram tiles.
  • Hough Deskewing: Calculates minimum bounding box of text contours and applies affine rotation straightening.
  • Adaptive Binarization: Converts multi-shade paper textures into clean monochrome pixels.
Interactive Before & After split slider available in the Verification Studio.
PILLAR 2: BILINGUAL OCR & NUMERAL NORMALIZER
  • Modular OCR Ensemble: Pluggable EasyOCR, Tesseract, PDF text stream, and fallback parsers.
  • Bilingual Hindi + English: Extracts character-level bounding boxes across mixed language land registers.
  • Native Devanagari Numeral Normalization: Converts handwritten Patwari Hindi digits to standard digital numbers:
Handwritten: २४५/२ (Khasra) ➔ Normalized: 245/2
Handwritten: ११२ (Khata) ➔ Normalized: 112
Handwritten: ४४१ (Mutation) ➔ Normalized: 441
Solves the #1 cause of transcription failures in Indian revenue portals.
Pillar 1 enhances faded scans via CLAHE and deskewing. Pillar 2 solves a massive real-world pain point: revenue officers write Khasra numbers in Devanagari script. Our engine automatically normalizes these numerals into digital Arabic digits with 96%+ accuracy.
